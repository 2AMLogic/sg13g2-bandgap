## Comparison to superseded record (issue #141)

The previous newest record,
[`records/20260826-152134-9b7a6de.md`](20260826-152134-9b7a6de.md), was
generated against `bandgap_core`'s pre-#134 `R1`. This experiment's own
`testbench/tb_closed_loop_iq.spice.tmpl` had never been updated for
issue #134's committed retune (PR #136: `R1` `rppd l=694.5u -> l=511u`)
even though the co-simulated core section was otherwise copied
verbatim — this record's own template fix (`l=694.5u -> l=511u` on the
`XR1` line) is part of this issue's change, not just a re-run.

**No material shift**: at the nominal corner (`typ`/27C/3.30V), `Iq`
moves from 2.85738e-05 A to 2.85791e-05 A (+0.019%); the same
negligible (~+0.018% to +0.019%) shift holds at the min and max points
of the 45-point range. `fb_v`/`sns1_v`/`sns2_v`/`det_v`/`i_mkfb_a`/
`dvsns_v` are all bit-identical between the two records. This is
expected: `Iq` sums `vdd`'s total drawn current, dominated by the
amplifier and mirror-leg bias currents, none of which route through
the `vref`/`cb3`/`Q3` dead-end branch `R1` sits in — `R1`'s own leg
current is only a few uA out of ~20-42 uA total, and its value does not
change that current (it only sets the voltage drop across itself).
Flagged explicitly per issue #141's acceptance criteria; the shift here
does not rise to "material" and this record's own headline Iq range
(1.99839e-05 A - 4.23783e-05 A) is materially the same as the
superseded record's.
