# Record 20261002-131827-d770c09a

- **Experiment**: trim-knob-jacobian (issue #267)
- **Claim**: the (level, drift) column of each candidate second trim
  knob, measured on the real trim-bearing closed loop about the
  code-128 / 27 C baseline, across {typ,bcs,wcs,sf,fs} x
  {2.97,3.30,3.63} V x {-40,27,125} C. At typ/3.30 V the measured
  offset-to-drift conversion ratios are: **A (as-built R1 ladder)
  0.33320**, **B (R2-side ladder) 0.32724**,
  **C (VBE/R CTAT injection) -0.15525**, **D (reference
  scaling) 0.00908**, against first-principles predictions
  dT/T = 0.32650 (A and B) and dVBE/VBE = -0.16691 (C).
  So: **the R2-side knob is parallel to the as-built ladder** (worst
  |ratio_B - ratio_A| across groups 0.00641, gate <=
  0.02) and supplies NO second degree of freedom, while
  **the CTAT injection is independent** (worst |ratio_C - ratio_A|
  0.48473, gate >= 0.30) and **reference
  scaling is level-only** (worst |ratio_D| 0.01122, gate <=
  0.05). Knob A's own ratio tracks dT/T to within
  2.545% at every group, which is why the coupling #267
  reports is a property of the knob's temperature SHAPE and not of
  the ladder's construction.
