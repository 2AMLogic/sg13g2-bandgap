# Resistor-fold area result (issue #173)

Before/after for the serpentine fold of every long poly-resistor bar in this
repo, and the top-level re-pack that fold enabled. This is the follow-up
measurement `measurements/2026-09-layout-area/` (issue #4 T1 item 5, PR #172)
called for: that analysis identified aspect ratio — one unfolded resistor bar
per cell — as the single mechanism behind **77.5%** of the assembled
footprint being whitespace, and this is the same measurement re-taken after
that mechanism was removed.

**This is not a spec change and not a conformance claim.**
`spec/porting-plan.md` §6's `Area | < 0.05 mm²` row is drafted and unratified
(#125, PR #128), and `measure_area.py` deliberately emits no verdict. The
numbers below are geometry measurements, exactly as the baseline record was.

Method, provenance and per-cell field definitions are unchanged — see
[`../2026-09-layout-area/README.md`](../2026-09-layout-area/README.md). Both
sides of every table below come from the same script
(`measurements/2026-09-layout-area/measure_area.py`), the same `klt stats`
call, and the same committed-GDS inputs; only the GDS differs.

- **Before** = `layout/**.gds` at `e884fde` (this branch's merge base).
- **After** = the same files as regenerated by this issue.
- `klt` for both sides: `klt 0.3.0+gc6dbf66c53c6.dirty` — the *same*
  binary, run twice, so no part of the delta below is tool drift.

## 1. Headline: assembled footprint

| top cell | before (µm²) | after (µm²) | before (mm²) | after (mm²) | reduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bandgap_top` (SG13G2) | 207,789.0 | 46,920.4 | 0.2078 | 0.0469 | **4.43×** |
| `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_top` | 321,089.3 | 47,635.6 | 0.3211 | 0.0476 | **6.74×** |

`sg13cmos5l_bandgap_top` is the cell issue #173 quantified: it went from
**6.4×** the drafted 0.05 mm² row to **0.95×** of it. `bandgap_top` — the
SG13G2 assembly, which landed after the baseline record was taken (#169 /
PR #170) and so has no entry in `area_record.json`'s committed baseline —
lands at 0.94× the same number.

Stated carefully, because the distinction is the whole point of the
no-verdict convention: *no pass is being claimed*. The row is a draft. What
is measured is that the mechanism the baseline analysis blamed accounted for
the gap it was blamed for.

## 3. Where the whitespace went

The baseline analysis's own headline metric — assembled footprint against the
sum of its own leaves' footprints:

| assembly | Σ leaf footprints (µm²) | assembled (µm²) | ratio | whitespace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_top`, before | 72,127.9 | 321,089.3 | 4.45× | 77.5% |
| `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_top`, after | 23,067.2 | 47,635.6 | **2.07×** | **51.6%** |
| `bandgap_top`, before | 72,141.8 | 207,789.0 | 2.88× | 65.3% |
| `bandgap_top`, after | 20,354.1 | 46,920.4 | **2.30×** | **56.6%** |

The residual ~52–57% is a **different** mechanism from the one this issue
removed, and it is worth naming rather than leaving as an unexplained
remainder:

1. **The inter-cell routing channel.** Both assemblies stack four (CMOS5L) or
   five (SG13G2) full-width horizontal buses above the cell row, plus the
   30 µm corridors between cells. On CMOS5L that channel is ~23 µm of the
   assembly's 94.75 µm height (~24% of the footprint) even after this issue
   pulled the bus stack down from 75–120 to 66–84; on SG13G2 it is ~24 µm of
   97.25 µm. That cost is roughly *fixed* in absolute terms, so shrinking the
   cells raised its share.
2. **Single-row placement.** The three leaves are still placed in disjoint
   x-ranges, left to right, with the tallest (`core`, 64.9 / 73.7 µm) setting
   the row height and the two shorter ones (33.8–53.5 µm) leaving their
   remaining height empty. Issue #173's own proposed item 2 — genuinely
   *interleaving* the placement into two rows — is **not** done here; see
   "Not done, and why" below.

Devices are still not the cost, and this measurement strengthens that: the
SG13G2 pair `bandgap_core` + `bandgap_startup` draws 15,349.6 µm² of
geometry after the fold against 15,978.8 µm² before. The fold conserves each
resistor's marked-core area exactly (see below); the 629 µm² that did
disappear is *routing* — the ~1.4 mm `det` trunk `bandgap_startup` needed to
reach the far end of its own resistor bar, which is now a 4.5 µm riser.

## 4. The fold conserves resistance exactly — checked, not assumed

Folding a resistor is only an area transform if it does not re-size the
device. That is enforced by construction in
`layout/_klayout_builder_base.py::fold_plan`, which derives the leg length
and inter-leg gap in whole nanometres such that
`legs*leg_len + (legs-1)*gap == l` exactly (see its docstring for the
derivation), and it is confirmed end-to-end by `klt extract`'s own recognised
device parameters — the same number, before and after, on every resistor the
deck recognises:

| device | cell | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R2` (`rppd`) | `bandgap_core` | `10751 Ω` | `10751 Ω` |
| `R1` (`rppd`) | `bandgap_core` | `66430 Ω` | `66430 Ω` |
| `RPU` (`rhigh`) | `bandgap_startup` | `1919368 Ω` | `1919368 Ω` |
| `R2` (`rppd`) | `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_core` | `11063 Ω` | `11063 Ω` |
| `R1` (`rppd`) | `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_core` | `84110 Ω` | `84110 Ω` |
| `RPU` (`rhigh`) | `sg13cmos5l_bandgap_startup` | `1919368 Ω` | `1919368 Ω` |

(Each equals `sheet_rho * l / w` at the schematic's own `l`/`w`:
`260 * 82.7 / 2 = 10751`, `260 * 511 / 2 = 66430`, `1360 * 1411.3 / 1 =
1919368`, `260 * 85.1 / 2 = 11063`, `260 * 647 / 2 = 84110`.)

Reproduce, either side:

```bash
klt extract layout/bandgap_startup/bandgap_startup.gds --deck sg13g2 -o /tmp/x.spice
grep '^R' /tmp/x.spice
```

`mismatch_count`/`error_count` and the per-category breakdown are identical
on both sides for every cell — no new mismatch class, and no old one
resolved either.

**Deck-drift caveat, stated because the committed reports show it.** The
three `sg13cmos5l-*` cells' *committed* reports at `e884fde` were produced
against an older `sg13cmos5l` deck build (`content_hash 9a4e18f2…`,
`klt_version 0.3.0`) that recognised no `rppd`/`rhigh` device at all. The
installed `klt` here (`0.3.0+gc6dbf66c53c6`) does, so the refreshed reports
in this branch show 2 more layout devices and 2 more layout nets on
`sg13cmos5l_bandgap_core` (29/28 rather than the committed 27/25). That is
**deck drift, not a fold regression** — which is why the "before" column
above is the pre-fold GDS re-run through the *current* binary, not the
committed report's own numbers. Both are recorded so the distinction is
auditable rather than asserted.

## 6. Not done, and why

- **Two-dimensional interleaving of the top-level placement** (issue #173's
  proposed item 2). The three leaves still occupy disjoint x-ranges. Both
  assemblies' inter-cell routing is documented as resting on exactly that
  invariant ("a vertical route drawn anywhere within one cell's own x-span
  can only ever collide with that same cell's own geometry"), which is what
  makes each net's crossings checkable one at a time under the single
  modelled routing metal the `sg13cmos5l` deck declares (`metals=((8, 0),)`,
  `vias=()`). Breaking it is a real re-verification of every riser column on
  both assemblies, and it is separable from the fold: the fold delivered
  4.4×/6.7×, and re-packing the row within the existing invariant delivered
  the rest. Tracked as **#177**.
- **Re-running the three PEX simulation suites.** The fold changed each
  layout's extracted *wire parasitics* (see §3 — routing got shorter), and
  the `sim/*-pex/` testbenches splice a hand-merged copy of that block.
  Refreshing them is mechanical but not automatic (the spliced block merges
  several extracted hub legs per device, by a rule the template does not
  state), so it is tracked as **#176** rather than guessed at here.
  No device parameter changed (§4), so no *device* geometry in those
  testbenches is stale.
